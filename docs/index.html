      Questions

      Is it free?

      Yes. Pulse Music is a free fan project. You need your own Spotify Premium account, because Spotify only lets Premium accounts play music in other apps.

      Will my playlists and likes be there?

      Yes. Pulse signs in to your Spotify account, so everything you change shows up in Spotify on your phone too, and the other way round.

      Spotify doesn't let me sign in.

      Spotify only lets a few accounts into each app. The sign-in screen then walks you through making your own free Spotify app key. It takes about two minutes.

      Who can see what I'm playing?

      Only people who follow you, and only while “Show my profile to other Pulse users” is on in your profile. Turn it off and nothing is shared.

      Does the AI DJ need the internet or an account?

      No account. The DJ's brain and voice run on your own computer. It still needs Spotify to play the songs it picks.

      How do updates work?

      Pulse checks for new versions in the background. When one is ready you get a “Restart now” button, or it installs itself the next time you close the app.

      Is there a Mac or phone version?

      Not yet. Pulse Music is made for Windows 10 and 11.
