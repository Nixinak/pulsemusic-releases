Spotify,painted bythe music.

Pulse Music is a free Spotify app for Windows. It takes its colours from the song that's playing, shows lyrics that follow the singer, has an AI DJ that talks between songs, and lets you listen along with friends.

Synced lyrics light up word by word, over a background painted from the cover. Click any line to jump there.

A DJ that knows your music.

Ask for a mood, an artist or a song. It builds a set from music you like, adds a few you haven't heard yet, and says a few words before the next song.

It understands where and when too: “classic czech songs”, “polský rap” or “90s rock” play exactly that. The orb moves with the beat of whatever is playing.

It runs on your own computer, so it's free and there's no account to make. The installer sets it up for you.

    Listen along with friends.

    Connect your Spotify account once and see what the people you follow on Spotify are playing, right on the Friends page. Follow anyone from their profile: when you follow each other, you're friends.

    If a friend uses Pulse too, you see what they're playing right now. One click puts you in the same song, at the same second, and you stay in step until you pick something else.

      Questions

      Is it free?

      Yes. Pulse Music is a free fan project. You need your own Spotify Premium account, because Spotify only lets Premium accounts play music in other apps.

      Will my playlists and likes be there?

      Yes. Pulse signs in to your Spotify account, so everything you change shows up in Spotify on your phone too, and the other way round.

      Spotify doesn't let me sign in.

      Spotify only lets a few accounts into each app. The sign-in screen then walks you through making your own free Spotify app key. It takes about two minutes.

      Who can see what I'm playing?

      Only people who follow you, and only while “Show my profile to other Pulse users” is on in your profile. Turn it off and nothing is shared.

      Does the AI DJ need the internet or an account?

      No account. The DJ's brain and voice run on your own computer. It still needs Spotify to play the songs it picks.

      Does Pulse count plays from my phone?

      Yes, while it runs. It starts quietly with Windows and keeps running in the tray, so plays on your phone and other devices count too. You can switch both off in Settings → System, and import your Spotify data download in Settings → Listening history.

      How do updates work?

      Pulse checks for new versions in the background. When one is ready you get a “Restart now” button, or it installs itself the next time you close the app.

      Is there a Mac or phone version?

      Not yet. Pulse Music is made for Windows 10 and 11.
