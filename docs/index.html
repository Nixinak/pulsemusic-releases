Up and running in two minutes.

  1. Download and open

    Get PulseMusic-Setup.exe and run it. You only do this once; new versions install themselves.

  2. Allow it on Windows

    If you see “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. The app isn't signed with a paid certificate, that's all.

  3. Sign in with Spotify

    Choose whether to add the AI DJ (about 5 GB, you can add it later), then sign in with your Spotify Premium account.

Spotify won't let you in? Spotify allows only a few accounts per app. The sign-in screen walks you through making your own free Spotify app key, which takes about two minutes.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Pulse Music is a free fan project. You need your own Spotify Premium account, because Spotify only lets Premium accounts play music in other apps.

Will my playlists and likes be there?

Yes. Pulse signs in to your Spotify account, so everything you change shows up in Spotify on your phone too, and the other way round.

How do updates work?

Pulse checks for new versions in the background. When one is ready you get a small “Restart now” button, or it installs the next time you close the app.

The AI DJ doesn't answer.

The DJ runs on your PC with Ollama. Open the DJ page and click “Set up the AI DJ for me”, and it downloads what it needs. The first answer can take a few seconds.

Is there a Mac or phone version?

Not yet. Pulse Music is made for Windows 10 and 11.
