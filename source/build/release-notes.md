## Pulse Music 1.6.8

- **Discover**: new music one song at a time, like a video feed. Each song plays its preview on its own (your music pauses meanwhile); like it, queue it, play it in full or skip it. In the sidebar.
- **Taste match**: on a friend's profile, how close your music is, the artists you share, and a blend of both your favourites to play.
- **Smart playlists** (Mix → Smart playlist): playlists made by rules, such as tempo (BPM), release years, how often you played a song, an artist or no explicit songs. Ready-made rules for workouts, forgotten likes, fresh and throwback.
- **Achievements** on Your stats, in bronze, silver and gold, with a toast when you earn one. Fun facts got a proper design with little charts.
- **Party votes**: guests (and you) give queued songs a thumbs up or down; the best go first and a flop gets voted off.
- **Shortcuts from any app**: play, skip, volume, like and show Pulse while you're in a game or the browser, with your own keys (Settings → General).
- **Listen along from Discord**: your Discord status has a Listen along button that opens Pulse on your friend's computer and plays what you play.
- **Lyrics**: they show up much faster (found lyrics are kept, the next song's are fetched ahead, Spotify's never hold LRCLIB's up). A song without lyrics keeps the cover until lyrics are really there. Share lines as a 9:16 Instagram story with the cover.
- **Friends**: Spotify Friend Activity reads the list the way the web player does when the direct way gives nothing; Pulse and Spotify friends are in one feed, with a Pulse badge on Pulse users. New design.
- **Now Playing**: a cartoon Poster (stickers, handwriting, doodles); the Visualizer follows the real sound much more closely; changing the look fades smoothly. Cassette and CD deck are gone.
- **Turntable**: grab the record again and flick it: it spins on by itself for a moment, scratching the song, then the music comes back.
- **Recap** opens right away instead of waiting on Spotify for pictures.
- **Fixes**: the mix animation no longer flickers; Settings no longer twitches when switching sections (no page in the app jumps sideways any more); buttons in Settings have room; the DJ page lost the extra song card.
