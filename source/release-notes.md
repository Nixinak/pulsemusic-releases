## Pulse Music 1.6.6

- **Scratch the record**: in the Vinyl look, grab the record with the mouse and spin it back and forth like a DJ; it scratches, follows your hand's speed and direction, and carries on spinning when you let go (with a push, if you gave it one). The record now speeds up and slows down with weight, like a real turntable. Switch scratching off in the Now Playing menu (three lines at the right edge).
- **A calmer start**: Pulse used to send a burst of requests to Spotify right after starting, which is exactly when an update restarts it. Now the first two minutes belong to what's on screen; background work (your history, the mixes) waits, and the DJ only gets its first set ready when you open its page.
