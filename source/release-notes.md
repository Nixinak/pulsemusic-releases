## Pulse Music 1.6.2

- **More playlists made for you, and longer ones**: six Daily Mixes, On Repeat and the new **Repeat Rewind** (songs you had on repeat earlier this year) every day; Discover Weekly, Release Radar and the new **Deep Cuts** and **Time Capsule** every Monday. Up to 40 songs each.
- **The DJ page, redone**: the song's cover fills a dark stage, the orb sits in the middle with your words under it like captions, and Next song is right there.
- **The DJ orb moves with the beat**: its ring is the live sound of the song and it jumps on every kick (on Windows Pulse listens to what the computer plays; only the levels are measured, nothing is recorded).
- **Better artist requests**: after "play Shimmi" the DJ stays with that artist and ones that sound like them, instead of random songs from a search.
- **Drag the queue**: everything coming up (your queue, the playlist or album, the DJ's show) can be dragged into a new order, and a double-click plays a song.
- **Profiles open much faster**: other people's pages are read in their own lane, stop as soon as Spotify has answered, and a profile you've seen before shows its playlists right away.
- **Shuffle on an artist works every time**, mixing the popular songs with the newest releases.
- The player bar no longer flickers when its size changes or while you scroll.
- The mix page lost its "Ctrl + Enter" hint and shortcut.
