## Pulse Music 1.6.7

- **New Settings**: categories on the left (General, Appearance, Playback, Now Playing, Lyrics, AI DJ, Integrations, Performance, Listening history, Account & updates) and a search box. New options: start page (Home or where you left off), app size (90–125 %), song notifications, a keyboard shortcut list, and Clear cache.
- **Performance or Quality**: Quality runs the background motion every frame (even while you scroll), with softer page changes and smoother lyrics. Performance stays as light as before. You can also turn hardware acceleration off (after a restart) if your graphics driver has trouble.
- **New Now Playing looks**: Cassette (the reels turn and the tape winds over as the song plays), Poster (the song as a printed gig poster) and Visualizer (the cover inside a ring that moves with the beat). Album cover is now the standard look; Classic is folded into it. The lock screen is darker.
- **Real scratching**: grabbing the record stops the music and scratches the song itself (its preview), and it sounds whenever you move it. The Device sounds switch is gone: the sounds are always on.
- **Lyrics**: they glide smoothly instead of stepping. A song without lyrics shows the big cover, and the next song with lyrics switches back on its own.
- **Your stats**: the hourly chart made way for fun facts: your longest streak, the song you played most in one day, your longest session, your biggest day, your hour and weekday, new artists and the song you skip most. Artist photos are cropped around the face, and imported months get their artist pictures and covers.
- **Playlist covers**: playlists Pulse makes (mixes, Made for you, merged playlists) get their own cover with four album covers and the name.
- **The AI DJ** understands more: "play the newest from Shimmi", "the oldest songs by…", "only Shimmi", "deep cuts", "hits", "something like…" (in Czech too).
- **Party** fills the whole panel; the mix animation got better and always has covers.
- **Fixes**: the volume bar closes again when the mouse leaves; the heart no longer flashes the previous song's like; leaving Recap brings back what you were playing; normal cursor in Recap; leaving full screen with Esc is smooth.
