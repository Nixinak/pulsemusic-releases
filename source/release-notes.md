## Pulse Music 1.7.5

- **Words**: one to three words share a row now (a long word gets a row to itself, a comma or the end of a line starts a new one), and the words land softly, without the tilt, sway and bump. The cover's colours fill the whole window, so there's no dark strip behind the buttons at the top any more.
- **Artists**: the monthly listeners show big under the name. When Spotify's full page doesn't load, Pulse reads them from the artist's link preview instead.
- **Achievements**: an award opens in the middle of the window, over everything.
- **Home**: the "today" pills under the greeting are gone.
