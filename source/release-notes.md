## Pulse Music 1.6.3

- **Looks for Now Playing**: move the pointer to the right edge of the screen and a small menu appears. The three lines open the looks:
  - **Classic**, the way it was.
  - **Album cover**: the cover, big and centre stage.
  - **Vinyl**: the record spins on a turntable with the cover as its label, and the arm lifts off when you pause.
  - **CD deck**: the disc turns under a clear lid, with a display showing the time and a scrolling title.
- **Full screen**: the button under the three lines puts Now Playing over the whole screen, taskbar included. Pulse remembers it, so next time Now Playing opens that way, and the window goes back to normal when you close it.
